📜 Ending Rules & Reliability Guidelines

• -son / -tion / -sion: 99% Feminine in French (e.g. la maison, la nation, la vision).

• -age / -ment / -eau: 95% Masculine in French (e.g. le garage, le gouvernement, le château).

⚠️ Common High-Frequency Exceptions

  • Le squelette (m) — ends in -ette but is masculine.
  • La mer (f) — ends in consonant but is feminine.
  • Le silence (m) — ends in -ence but is masculine.
