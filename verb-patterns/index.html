Verb Pattern Classification Guide

Master verbs faster by learning pattern groups rather than memorizing individual verbs in isolation.

🇬🇧 English: Pattern Group 1 — No Change (V1 = V2 = V3)

25+ Verbs

Base form, Past Simple, and Past Participle remain identical.

🇫🇷 French: 1st Conjugation (-er verbs)

Regular (90% of verbs)

Standard present endings: -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent.

Examples: parler, aimer, chanter, regarder, danser.

🇮🇹 Italian: -isc- Inchoative Verbs (-ire)

30+ Verbs

Insert -isc- in singular and 3rd person plural present indicative.

Examples: finire (finisco), capire (capisco), preferire (preferisco).

🇷🇺 Russian: Aspectual Pairs (НСВ / СВ)

Systemic Feature

Imperfective (process/habit) vs Perfective (completed result).

Examples: читать / прочитать, делать / сделать, писать / написать.

🇬🇷 Greek: Contracted Verbs Class A (-άω / -ώ)

40+ Verbs

Aorist (past simple) stem shifts with -ησ- affix.

Examples: μιλώ (μίλησα), αγαπώ (αγάπησα), απαντώ (απάντησα).
